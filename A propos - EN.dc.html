Who we are

Presentation & Our vision

Based in Geneva, in the heart of the banking district, RealXpert is a company specialising in real estate and artificial intelligence. Officially launched in 2026, after several years of research, development and expertise built up since 2022, the company was born of a strong conviction: to give everyone access to the highest level of real estate expertise, at any time, to make the right decisions in a high-stakes real estate project.

Backed by a private funding round, RealXpert is today building one of Switzerland's most advanced real estate platforms in artificial intelligence applied to decision support. Our technology was designed to support individuals at every stage of their real estate journey, giving them access to reliable, verified information that is continuously updated and tailored to the specifics of their region.

Our ambition is to allow everyone with a real estate project to benefit, at any moment, from a level of expertise comparable to — or even greater than — that of the market's best professionals. Whether to buy, sell or simply better understand one's situation, RealXpert provides the information, analyses and recommendations needed to make the right decisions at the right time.

Because a real estate project is often one of the most important financial decisions of a lifetime, we designed a platform available 24/7, easy to use, intuitive and value-generating at every interaction. Our goal is to help our users avoid costly mistakes, make the right choices, carry out their transactions in the best possible conditions and obtain the best possible final price for their project.

Our origins

Why we created RealXpert

Buying or selling a property is no ordinary decision. Every choice can have significant consequences: setting a price, choosing an agency, negotiating an offer, understanding your financing, anticipating taxation or deciding on the right moment to act.

Yet individuals often have to move forward with scattered information, conflicting opinions and an incomplete understanding of the consequences of their decisions. Many don't know what to check, whom to trust or which direction to favour.

RealXpert was born of this reality: in a real estate project, the problem isn't a lack of information, but the difficulty of getting the right information, at the right time, in the right context.

Our goal is to reduce this uncertainty and give everyone the means to make better-prepared, clearer and safer decisions.

Our mission

What we want to change in real estate

For a long time, access to high-level real estate expertise depended mainly on your network, your budget, the right contact or the moment when you managed to ask the right question.

We believe this should no longer be the case. Every owner, buyer or seller should be able to understand what is at stake in their project, compare their options, identify the risks and have reliable guidance before committing.

With RealXpert, we want to make this expertise more accessible, more available and more tailored to each situation. Users don't just receive a general answer: they benefit from a context-aware analysis, built around their project, their objectives, their local market and the decisions they have to make.

Our ambition is to allow everyone to move forward with the level of clarity demanded by decisions that often involve several hundred thousand — even several million — francs.

Our vision

Our vision for the future

Real estate is becoming ever more complex: price movements, market pressures, financing requirements, taxation, agency models, the varying quality of professionals, a growing volume of information and the rapid development of digital tools.

In this context, making a good decision is no longer just about finding information. You have to know how to interpret it, compare it, place it in context and understand what it really means for your own project.

Our vision is to make RealXpert the reference platform in Switzerland for supporting individuals in their important real estate decisions.

We want to offer a space where every user can ask their questions, understand their situation, track their project, prepare their next steps and access the right experts when it becomes necessary.

RealXpert was built for one thing: to help everyone make the right real estate decisions, at the right time, with the right guidance.
