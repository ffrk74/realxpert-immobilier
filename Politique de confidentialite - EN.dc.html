Privacy Policy

1. Who are we?

1.1 Data controller

The realxpert.ch website and the RealXpert services are operated by Urech & Partners Sàrl, controller of your personal data.

For any question relating to the protection of your personal data, you can contact us at contact@realxpert.ch.

1.2 Purpose of the privacy policy

This privacy policy explains how RealXpert collects and processes your personal data in connection with the use of its services.

2. What data do we process?

In order to provide you with the RealXpert services, we process the personal data you provide to us as well as certain data generated during your use of our services.

2.1 Data you provide to us

When you create an account or use RealXpert, we may in particular process:

  • your identification and contact data, such as your last name, first name, email address and telephone number;
  • the information you provide concerning your property project, your situation, your needs and your objectives;
  • the personal, financial or asset-related information necessary for the services you choose to use;
  • the content of your conversations and exchanges with RealXpert;
  • the documents or other content you may choose to send us.

Your identification and contact data linked to your account — in particular your last name, first name, email address and telephone number — is kept separately from the information processed by the artificial intelligence and is not directly associated with it.

RealXpert uses only the information you choose to provide to it and does not seek personal data concerning you from external sources.

We invite you to provide only the information necessary for the use of the services concerned.

2.2 Data collected during your use

When you use RealXpert, certain data may be collected automatically, in particular:

  • technical and connection data;
  • information relating to the use of our services;
  • information collected by means of cookies or similar technologies.

This data is used in particular to enable the operation, security and improvement of our services.

3. Why do we use your data?

3.1 Providing the RealXpert services

We use the information you provide to us in order to provide you with the RealXpert services, in particular to:

  • understand and support your property project;
  • provide you with the information, analyses and recommendations requested;
  • adapt the services to your situation and your needs;
  • manage your account and your access to the services;
  • follow up on your requests.

When you purchase additional access to the RealXpert services, the necessary data may also be processed in order to manage your purchase and access to the services concerned.

3.2 Use of artificial intelligence

Certain RealXpert features rely on artificial intelligence technologies.

In this context, the information you provide and which is necessary for the provision of the service may be processed in order to provide you with the answers, information, analyses and recommendations requested in connection with your property project.

As indicated above, your identification and contact data linked to your account is kept separately from the information processed by the artificial intelligence and is not directly associated with it.

3.3 Operation and improvement of RealXpert

We may also process certain data where this is necessary in order to:

  • ensure the operation and security of RealXpert;
  • provide assistance to users;
  • maintain and improve our services;
  • communicate with you regarding your account or the services used;
  • comply with our legal obligations.

3.4 Communications and support for your project

RealXpert may use your contact details to communicate with you in connection with your use of its services and your property project.

We may in particular contact you in order to respond to your requests, support you in your project, follow up on the services used, inform you about the support options offered by RealXpert and, where relevant, propose services or professionals able to meet your needs.

We may also send you advice, information, news, recommendations and communications concerning RealXpert, its services or your property project, under the conditions provided for by the applicable legislation.

Where your consent is required for certain communications, these are sent to you on that basis. You can withdraw your consent or object to the communications concerned at any time, in particular by means of the unsubscribe link where it is available or by contacting us at contact@realxpert.ch.

Communications necessary for the management of your account, the provision of the services, the follow-up of a request or the relationship with RealXpert may continue to be sent to you where they are necessary for those purposes.

3.5 Legal bases

Where the applicable legislation requires it, we process your data on the legal basis corresponding to the purpose concerned.

Depending on the circumstances, the processing may in particular be necessary for the provision of a service you request, for compliance with a legal obligation or for our legitimate interests, in particular to ensure the operation, security and improvement of our services.

Where your consent is required, the processing concerned is based on it.

4. With whom may your data be shared?

RealXpert discloses your personal data to third parties only where this is necessary for the provision of its services, for supporting you or for compliance with its legal obligations.

4.1 Service providers

RealXpert calls on external providers to carry out certain functions necessary for the provision and operation of its services.

Depending on the services used, certain data may in particular be processed by providers involved in:

  • hosting and technical services;
  • artificial intelligence services;
  • payment processing;
  • sending communications;
  • security, analysis and improvement of the services.

Only the data necessary for the performance of the services concerned is disclosed or made accessible to these providers.

4.2 Experts and partners

In connection with your property project, RealXpert may offer to put you in touch with professionals able to support you, in particular property experts and professionals.

Where you wish to benefit from such an introduction or from a service requiring the involvement of a professional, the information necessary for supporting you may be disclosed to them.

4.3 Legal obligations

RealXpert may also disclose personal data where this is necessary in order to comply with a legal obligation, respond to a request from a competent authority or protect its rights in accordance with the applicable legislation.

4.4 Changes to the company

In the event of a reorganisation, merger, acquisition, disposal or transfer of all or part of RealXpert’s business, the personal data concerned may be transferred to the new controller or to the entity concerned, in compliance with the applicable data protection legislation.

5. Where and for how long is your data kept?

5.1 Storage and processing of data

The personal data kept directly by RealXpert is hosted in Switzerland.

Depending on the services used and the providers necessary for their provision, certain data may also be processed in Switzerland, in countries of the European Union or in the United States.

Where data is processed abroad, RealXpert ensures that its processing complies with the requirements of the applicable data protection legislation and, where necessary, is based on the appropriate safeguards provided for by it.

5.2 Retention period

We keep your personal data for as long as it is necessary for the provision of the RealXpert services and for the purposes for which it was collected.

The retention period depends in particular on the nature of the data, the services used, the duration of your relationship with RealXpert and any applicable legal obligations.

Where the data is no longer necessary, it is deleted or anonymised, subject to the data we must keep longer by reason of a legal obligation or another legitimate ground.

5.3 Data security

RealXpert implements appropriate technical and organisational measures in order to protect your personal data against loss, unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or any other unauthorised use.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

RealXpert may use cookies and similar technologies in order to ensure the operation and security of its services, remember certain preferences and better understand their use in order to improve them.

Where this is required, you can accept, refuse or change your preferences concerning cookies that are not necessary for the operation of the service.

You can also manage certain cookies directly from your browser settings.

7. Your rights and contacting us

7.1 Your rights

You may, under the conditions provided for by the applicable legislation, in particular request:

  • access to the personal data concerning you;
  • the rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • the deletion of your personal data;
  • the handover or transmission of certain of your data where this right is applicable;
  • the restriction of certain processing or to object to it where these rights are applicable;
  • the withdrawal of your consent where processing is based on it.

You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority where the legal conditions are met.

7.2 Exercising your rights and contacting us

For any question relating to your personal data or to exercise your rights, you can contact:

Urech & Partners Sàrl
contact@realxpert.ch

In order to protect your data, we may ask you to confirm your identity where this is necessary before acting on your request.

7.3 Changes to the privacy policy

RealXpert may amend this privacy policy in order to take account in particular of changes to its services or of the applicable legal requirements.

The version in force is the one published on RealXpert.
