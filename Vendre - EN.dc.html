The real risk

A bad decision can cost you dearly without you even realising it

The final price of your sale depends on a series of decisions

A sale can lose value without any obvious major mistake being made. A misread valuation, an unsuitable starting price, an agency chosen on the wrong criterion, an ill-suited broker, a weak sales strategy, a poorly calibrated reaction to the market or a badly handled negotiation can each reduce the final price.

Taken separately, these choices may seem secondary. Added together, they can represent several tens of thousands of francs on your sale.

The hardest part is that you won’t always immediately know which decision actually reduced the value obtained. A missing piece of information, insufficient analysis, a too-quick choice or an unchecked detail can have consequences that only become visible later.

RealXpert helps you step back before these decisions, while it is still possible to compare, check, adjust your strategy or defend your position.

Your point of reference

The risk is also not knowing what to check

You probably don’t sell a property every day. Yet, over a few weeks or months, you’ll have to make decisions about price, agencies, contracts, marketing, buyers or negotiation, with sometimes considerable sums at stake.

There will be a lot of information. Opinions may diverge. And you won’t always have everything you need to immediately gauge the consequences of a choice.

So the risk isn’t only making a bad decision. It’s also not knowing that an important detail should have been checked before deciding.

RealXpert becomes your point of reference throughout the sale: as soon as a doubt arises, you can ask your question, weigh what you’re being offered and check your decision before committing.

Your interests first

Defend your interests every time a decision can change the price of your sale

A sale involves your wealth and sometimes tens of thousands of francs riding on a few decisions.

A broker can be competent, but every professional also has their own interests: winning a mandate, selling fast, closing. Some recommendations must therefore be checked in your own interest.

What may seem normal… but cost you dearly

A high valuation can mainly serve to win a mandate.
A price cut can mainly help achieve a faster sale.
A slightly low offer can seem acceptable to speed up the transaction.

RealXpert helps you keep your own point of reference: understand what you’re being offered, ask the right questions and check that each decision genuinely serves your interests before committing.

Two complementary roles

Your broker handles the sale. RealXpert helps you step back and make the right choices

Your broker markets your property, manages viewings, buyers and negotiation. But throughout the sale, certain important decisions remain yours: what price to set, which mandate to sign, whether to follow a recommendation, change the price, accept an offer or keep negotiating?

RealXpert lets you step back at the moment you have to decide. You understand what you’re being offered, check the important points and compare your options with an independent perspective to decide in your own interest.

And each time your situation evolves, you can return to RealXpert to check before deciding and keep defending your sale price as best you can.
