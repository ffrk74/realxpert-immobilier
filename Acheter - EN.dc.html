The real risk is overpaying without knowing you could have done better

What you actually pay often depends on the decisions made before the purchase

In a property purchase, you don’t need to make a huge mistake to pay far more than necessary. An offer made too quickly out of fear of losing the property, underestimated renovation work, a misjudged value or a negotiation stopped too soon can each add up to CHF 30,000, CHF 70,000 or more on a major transaction.

The hardest part is that once the purchase is signed, you won’t always know you could have secured better terms. RealXpert helps you check the right things before acting, understand what can truly influence the price and decide while it’s still possible to compare, negotiate, adjust your offer or walk away.

Your point of reference

When it’s time to decide, you’re often alone with the decision

During your purchase, you’ll receive information and opinions from many people.

The broker knows the property and represents the sale.
The bank analyses your financing.
The seller naturally defends their own interests.
Your friends and family give you their opinion.

But when it comes to deciding whether the property truly fits, whether its price is sound, how much to offer, how far to negotiate or whether to walk away, you’re the one who has to decide. Outside opinions can help, but they aren’t always enough to know the right decision for your situation.

A misjudged price, an offer that’s too high or a decision made under pressure can cost you a great deal of money without your realising it right away.

RealXpert becomes your own point of reference throughout the purchase: a neutral space to check what really matters, weigh your options and make your decisions with the right information before committing.

A new property appears, a viewing is scheduled, an offer must be made or the seller comes back with a counter-proposal: each new element can change the decision to make.
